Attendance & Points Scoring Rules
• Parade Fall-in: Opening (1 pt) + Dismissal (1 pt). Minimum 80% attendance required for One Year Service.
• Uniform Inspection: Scored 0 – 6 points each parade day based on uniform correctness and neatness.
• Band Rehearsal: Saturday 9:30 AM – 11:30 AM (1 pt opening + 1 pt dismissal).
• Official Leave: Granted with valid reason or letter prior to parade.

Child Safety & Privacy Governance
1. Strict Data Minimization 1st Petaling Jaya Boys' Brigade adheres strictly to data minimization principles under the Malaysian Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010) and COPPA guidelines. We only collect the minimal information necessary to operate company records (member full name, login email, section, platoon, rank, and approximate age). We strictly do NOT collect National Identity Card (NRIC/MyKad/MyKid) numbers, home residential addresses, or minors' personal phone numbers.
2. Mandatory Parental / Legal Guardian Consent For all minor members aged 11 and under (Pre-Junior and Junior Sections), verifiable parental or legal guardian consent is strictly required. Consent is captured via an electronic signature audit trail, logging the guardian's name, contact details, timestamp, and explicit affirmation.
3. Privacy-First Default Permissions (Media & Photos) We believe in privacy-by-default. Photography and media release permissions are optional and disabled by default. If a parent chooses not to opt-in, their child will not be featured in public photo galleries, social media posts, or promotional materials.
4. Purpose Limitation & Zero Third-Party Sharing Personal data is used exclusively for BB parade records, attendance tracking, awards/badge achievements, and official company communications. Information is never sold, leased, or shared with commercial entities or third-party advertisers.
5. Parental Right to Access & Erasure In accordance with Section 30 of the Malaysian PDPA 2010, parents and legal guardians have the full legal right to inspect, correct, export, or request the permanent deletion (erasure) of their child's account and personal data at any time by contacting Company Officers or the Company Captain.
